The workflow is the product.

Personal OS is not trying to be another generic AI chat. It starts with the messy middle of client work and keeps the context usable.

1

Capture the client

Keep contacts, status, deals, and project notes around the account.

2

Add meeting notes

Drop rough notes in and turn them into decisions, tasks, and open questions.

3

Ask with context

Ask what happened, what changed, what is blocked, or what should happen next.

4

Run AI workflows

Use multi-step AI workflows for research, analysis, writing, and proposal prep.

More than notes plus chat.

Who this is for.

The early version is intentionally focused on people who manage multiple client relationships and repeatedly reconstruct context.

Consultants

Recurring meetings, proposal prep, follow-ups, and client-specific memory.

Agencies

Project notes, revisions, contacts, deadlines, and next actions across accounts.

Fractional operators

Lots of context switching, owners, open loops, and delivery work to keep moving.
